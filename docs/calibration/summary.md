Frequency/tier counts and daily trajectories are written alongside this report. Zero-frequency events are not evidence of a bug: third-party, achievement, distress and other missing contexts are not fabricated. Natural runs select from the 90 core definitions and do not exercise every core opportunity or any of the 48 tradition-specific gathering episodes. The coverage table includes all 138 definitions, with unexercised tradition entries explicitly zero. Controlled outcome probes include all 138 but bypass eligibility and do not apply consequences. No work time or TPS is measured; jobs, pathing, social memories, actual activity distribution and player interruption require live validation. Recovery mode deliberately changes profiles and opinions after day 150 to test whether entrenched histories can improve; it does not model an organic personality change. Synthetic-hourly mode adds passive bond updates but does not simulate physical activity jobs. All initial pawns remain at their starting age and colony presence to isolate social rules.
