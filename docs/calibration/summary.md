Frequency/tier counts and daily trajectories are written alongside this report. Zero-frequency events are not evidence of a bug: third-party, achievement, distress and other missing contexts are not fabricated. These runs do not exercise all 90 definitions. No work time or TPS is measured; jobs, pathing, social memories, actual activity distribution and player interruption require live validation. Recovery mode deliberately changes profiles and opinions after day 150 to test whether entrenched histories can improve; it does not model an organic personality change. Synthetic-hourly mode adds passive bond updates but does not simulate physical activity jobs. All initial pawns remain at their starting age and colony presence to isolate social rules.
