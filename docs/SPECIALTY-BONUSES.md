# Specialty bonuses

A specialty is mechanically meaningful only while the pawn is qualified **and assigned** to that exact specialty. The Careers UI exposes the bonus in the role tooltip. This prevents a pawn from collecting every bonus they have ever qualified for while assigned elsewhere.

## Education

Every subject specialist gives 15% better delivery when leading a class in their assigned subject. Their existing assistant behavior remains: sustained specialist assistance gives one bounded 10% class-delivery bonus and the specialist can take over an interrupted matching class.

## Community Care

- Counselor: +10 percentage points to supportive-talk success and a chance to encourage reconciliation.
- Surgeon: specialist implant and post-operation follow-ups improve one quality band, capped at Excellent.
- Midwife: pregnancy and childbirth follow-ups improve one quality band, capped at Excellent; attended births leave a positive memory.
- Veterinarian: animal examinations improve one quality band, capped at Excellent; animal tending and surgery build veterinary experience.

These bonuses do not replace or silently alter vanilla surgery outcomes.

## Cooking and Hospitality

- Artisan Chef: Artistic contributes to presentation and the signature-experiment failure chance falls to 8%.
- Bartender: +10 tavern service and +10 percentage points to the satisfied-customer tip chance.
- Innkeeper: +10 inn service and +10 guest comfort.

## Crafting and Workshops

Weaponsmith, Armorsmith, Couturier and Outfitter improve matching quality by one band up to Excellent and earn bounded shop premiums. Mechanist goods receive a 20% presentation/value premium because many mechanical products do not carry RimWorld quality levels.

Wil's Treasury currently has no level-three specialty; Treasurer and Tax Collector therefore receive no fabricated specialty bonus. If Treasury gains branching specialties later, they should use this same assigned-specialty rule.
