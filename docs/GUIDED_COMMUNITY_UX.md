# Guided Community, careers and Treasury UI

This change replaces the role-by-role assignment list with a colonist-first career map. Each loaded module supplies its existing career/role definitions; the map reads saved qualifications and progress, groups entry and qualified roles above their specialties, and lets locked roles be inspected. The detail pane shows skill, general and specialty requirements, explains how experience is earned, and provides a named assignment action. Multiple assignments and retained/legacy qualifications remain supported. The pawn Family tab and service staffing links open the same screen.

Community Overview lists earned advanced qualifications that are not assigned, instead of module installation status. Module status stays in Settings. Professional working seats are omitted from the political-post summary using the existing IsProfessional rule. Family cards show crests, residents and existing perspective-filtered concerns; the family heading and a named View action open the full family window. Expensive readiness and concern summaries are cached and refreshed after relevant changes or a short interval.

Education explains disabled classes, a queued request, unavailable instructors, lack of suitable pupils, lack of a reachable classroom, or the normal scheduling wait. Care shows whether rounds are disabled, idle, planned or running, plus recorded examination/refusal counts. Staff links open career management. Care start actions use the existing routines and respect the existing office authority check.

Treasury Overview separates available community funds, wages awaiting payday and the next payday. Previous unpaid wages and missing backing remain visible warnings. Ownership, physical silver, all-account backing and debts are available under Show silver accounting. Staffing links go to the relevant career role, and the treasury-space section explains zone designation and its purpose.

Assets & policies contains the income-share draft and Apply action. Moving the slider alone does not update policy. The setting still applies to future rewards and shared communal trade profit; it does not rewrite prior balances, wages or debts. Tracked asset quantities explicitly describe loose/stored stacks on the current map, excluding inventories and caravans. Stop tracking never deletes items. Initialization is identified from the original reconciliation memo and displayed as a starting balance; other corrections retain their reason. The existing full transaction history remains available.

No domain models, persistence/Scribe code, package IDs, qualification thresholds, experience awards, wage/tax policy rules, household settlement or inheritance code changed.
