# Wil's Family Life — Role Icon Pack

Original role-badge assets for Wil's Family Life.

## Contents

- `SVG_Masters_128/`: editable 128×128 vector masters.
- `PNG_64_GameReady/`: transparent 64×64 PNGs for RimWorld.
- `PNG_128_Preview/`: larger raster exports for documentation and review.
- `WFL_Role_Icons_Preview.png`: labeled contact sheet.

The four `protector_*` designs are interchangeable variants for the House Protector office:

1. `protector_eye` — vigilance (recommended default)
2. `protector_spears` — martial defense
3. `protector_tower` — watchfulness/readiness
4. `protector_house` — protection of the household

The assets use a transparent background, warm ivory fill, charcoal outline, and muted gold accent. They are designed to remain legible when rendered at approximately 14–22 UI pixels. Secret roles such as conspirator or criminal participant are intentionally not represented.

These original assets may be used, modified, recolored, and redistributed with Wil's Family Life.
